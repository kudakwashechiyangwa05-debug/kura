Privacy Policy for Kura

Last updated: 29 October 2026.

Kura ("the app") is published by Kura, Zimbabwe. Contact: WhatsApp +263 776 213 743, email kudakwashechiyangwa05@gmail.com.

What information the app handles

Kura helps you keep business records: products, sales, customers, suppliers, expenses, debts and notes. You type this information into the app yourself.

Where it is stored

All information is stored only on your own device. Kura does not have user accounts, does not upload your records to any server, and does not send your data to us or to anyone else.

What we collect

The app itself does not collect, receive, sell or share any personal information, business records, location, contacts, analytics or advertising identifiers. The only information we receive is what you choose to send us on WhatsApp when buying a plan or asking for help (see below).

Backups

You can create a backup file from Settings. The backup file is created on your device and you choose where to save or send it (for example WhatsApp or Google Drive). Once you share a backup, that service's own privacy policy applies to the copy you shared.

Paid plans and activation

Kura works without an account. To buy a paid plan you choose to give us or a Kura agent (for example on WhatsApp) your Install ID (a random code created on your device that does not identify you), your business name and your payment reference. We use these only to confirm your payment and send your activation code, and we keep a record of the sale (Install ID, plan, dates, phone number used, amount) for accounting and to help you if you change phones. The activation code is checked on your device; the app does not contact us to do this.

Staff PINs

If you turn on staff PINs, the PINs are stored on your device in a scrambled (hashed) form only.

Camera

If your device supports it, Kura can use the camera to scan product barcodes. Images from the camera are processed on your device only and are never stored or sent anywhere.

Contacts and phone numbers

Customer and supplier phone numbers you enter stay on your device. When you tap a WhatsApp button (reminders, receipts, statements, summaries), the message opens in WhatsApp on your device; sending it is up to you.

Children

Kura is a business tool and is not directed at children.

Deleting your data

You can erase all data at any time in Settings, or by uninstalling the app. Because we hold no copy, we cannot recover data for you. Please make backups.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
