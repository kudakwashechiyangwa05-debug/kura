Questions

Do I need data to use Kura?

No. Kura works fully offline. You only need data to download it, to send WhatsApp messages and to make online backups.

Where are my records kept?

On your phone only. We cannot see them. Make a backup often (Settings, Back up now) and send it to yourself on WhatsApp or Google Drive.

What happens when my plan ends?

Kura goes back to the free plan. Nothing is deleted or locked. Only the paid features switch off until you renew.

I changed my phone. Do I lose my plan?

Restore your backup on the new phone, then send us your new Install ID on WhatsApp. We move the rest of your plan to the new phone once.

Can my workers use Kura?

Yes. Kura Business gives each worker their own PIN and role, so you can see who sold what and pay commission.

Is Kura a ZIMRA fiscal device?

No. Kura helps you keep good records, which makes tax time easier, but it does not file tax for you.

Android says the app is from an unknown source

That is normal for apps downloaded from a website. Tap Settings, allow your browser to install apps, then go back and tap Install. Kura will also be on Google Play soon.
